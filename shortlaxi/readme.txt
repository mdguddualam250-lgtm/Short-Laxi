A premium, mobile-first editorial block theme for Shortlaxi, a global English-language publication, with app-style content discovery.

== Description ==

Shortlaxi publishes written articles with an app-style discovery experience:

* Sticky top bar with logo and a prominent search field (compact search toggle on phones).
* Sidebar with Home, Explore, Latest Articles, Trending, Bookmarks and Categories. Collapses to an icon rail on desktop; opens as a drawer on tablets and phones.
* Front page: category chips, featured story (newest sticky post), top-stories grid, ranked trending row, bookmarks shelf and a paginated latest-articles feed.
* 16:9 article cards with category, title, excerpt, date, reading time and a bookmark button.
* Article pages: large featured image, byline with avatar and reading time, share controls, automatic table of contents, related articles, author box and comments.
* Dedicated templates for pages with the slugs explore, latest, trending and bookmarks.
* Dark "Midnight" palette by default with a light "Daylight" style variation. System fonts only.

Theme blocks: Category Chips, Share Controls, Table of Contents, Bookmark Button and Bookmarked Articles.
Bookmarks are stored in the reader's browser; no accounts or plugins are required.
